Privacy Policy

Fire Momentum

Last updated: 07 Nov 2025

Overview

Fire Momentum is designed to help you track focus and daily momentum while keeping your data on your device. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

No Data Collection

  • No account creation and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or profiling.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, or other device data.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

On-Device Only

All entries you create (e.g., daily values, timers, notes) remain on your device. If you enable system backups (such as iCloud device backup), Apple may back up this data on your behalf under their terms and privacy policies.

No Ads or Monetization

The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.

Children’s Privacy

Fire Momentum does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children. Because no information is collected, there is nothing to disclose or delete.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, we will update the date above and reflect the changes in the app. Any future change that adds data collection will be opt-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please reach out via the contact details on the app’s App Store listing or the in-app Settings → Privacy section.

© 2025 Fire Momentum. This policy is intentionally concise and written in plain language.
